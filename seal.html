For reports, research papers, and data records. Scan it to open the signed Passport.

What a seal means—and does not mean

The seal is a doorway to a signed Passport. It helps a person find the record behind AI-assisted work.

A seal alone does not prove that a report is true, that a person approved it, or that it will be accepted by a court or regulator. Open the Passport and check the issued record.

C2PA can carry signed history with a digital file. A DeBacco Nexus Passport can add the governed AI-work record behind it.
