Methodology — energy, water, and carbon estimates

Energy (Joules)

Per-token energy derived from published GPU power measurements (NVIDIA A100/H100) at declared utilization. Gate + target inference summed. 1 token ≈ 0.0054 J (opus tier at 340 g CO₂/kWh grid).

Water (mL)

Cooling water estimate from published data-center PUE and water-usage-effectiveness (WUE) ratios. Northern California regional coefficients applied.

CO₂e (grams)

Energy × grid carbon intensity. Greek grid: 340 g/kWh (EU average). Northern California: 210 g/kWh. Declared at issue time.

Prevented Energy

When a governed gate withholds an inference (WITHHOLD or CRISIS_ROUTE), the target model's energy is prevented — never consumed. That prevention is the environmental fiduciary duty in action.
