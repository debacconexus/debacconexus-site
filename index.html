How it works

Three clear outcomes.

The same simple language follows the Passport wherever it travels.

✓

Green — released

The request met the required conditions. The Passport is issued with the record of the AI work and its declared resource use.

▮

Yellow — review needed

The request needs a person to decide. The work stays paused until the concern is resolved.

◼

Red — stopped

The request did not meet a required condition. The target AI work does not proceed.

Some rules require certain AI-made or AI-changed content to be clearly marked. The exact rule depends on the place and use.
